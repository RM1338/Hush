# Recording guide (own data, v1)

These clips teach and test Hush in real conditions. Only the lip dots are kept, and each video is deleted once its dots are extracted (D-43).

## Before recording
- **Get written consent from every person**, and from a parent if they are under 18. The note says: videos of your mouth are used only to train and test this school project, are kept on one laptop, and are deleted after processing.
- Use the phone's **front camera** at normal video quality, held by hand the way you'd use the app.

## How
- Mouth each phrase **silently**, **10 times**, with a short pause between takes. One phrase per video.
- Record in **2 sessions**: one indoors under normal light, one somewhere different (by a window, in dim light, or outdoors).
- Name each file `<person>_<phrase-id>_<session>.mp4`, for example `ronel_p07_s1.mp4`.
- Copy the files to `data/own/<person>/`.

## Junk clips (for testing "none of these")
These have no phrase, so a correct result is "none". Record about 30 seconds of each per person, in one video per item:
- **j01:** chewing
- **j02:** smiling and laughing
